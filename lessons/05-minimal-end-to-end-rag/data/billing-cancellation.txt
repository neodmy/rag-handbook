Cancelling your Acme Cloud subscription

You can cancel your Acme Cloud plan at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel
plan. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, so you
keep access to all paid features until that date. We do not cancel mid-cycle.

When you cancel an annual plan, you remain on the paid tier until the annual term
ends; we do not pro-rate the remaining months automatically. If you cancel a
monthly plan, you keep access until the end of the current month.

After cancellation your account is downgraded to the Free tier. Your data is kept
for 30 days so you can reactivate without losing anything; after 30 days, projects
above the Free-tier limit are archived and then deleted.
