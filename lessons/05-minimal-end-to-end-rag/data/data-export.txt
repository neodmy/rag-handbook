Exporting your data

You can export your Acme Cloud data at any time from Settings > Data > Export. We
generate a downloadable archive containing your projects as JSON plus any uploaded
files. Large exports are prepared in the background and we email you a link when the
archive is ready.

The download link is valid for 24 hours. Exports include your own data only; they
do not include data from workspaces owned by other accounts, even if you have access
to them.

There is no limit on how often you can export. Exporting does not change or delete
anything in your account.
