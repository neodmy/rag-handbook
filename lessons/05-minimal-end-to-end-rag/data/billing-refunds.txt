Refund policy

Acme Cloud offers a refund only within 14 days of the original purchase or of a
renewal charge. To request one, contact support@acme.example within that window
and we will refund the most recent payment to the original payment method.

Outside the 14-day window we do not issue refunds for the current period. When you
cancel, you are not charged again, but the payment for the period you are already
in is not returned. Refunds are not pro-rated for unused time within a period.

Refunds typically take 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement, depending
on your bank or card issuer.
