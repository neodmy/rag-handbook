Plans and tiers

Acme Cloud has three tiers. The Free tier includes up to 3 projects and community
support. The Pro tier adds unlimited projects, email support, and daily backups.
The Team tier adds shared workspaces, role-based access control, and a 99.9% uptime
service-level agreement.

You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from Settings > Billing. Upgrades take
effect immediately and are billed pro-rata for the rest of the current period.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period.

Pro and Team are billed either monthly or annually; annual billing is discounted
compared to paying month by month.
